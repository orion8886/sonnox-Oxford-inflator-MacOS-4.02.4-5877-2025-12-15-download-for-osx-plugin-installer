IK Multimedia Releases

[Windows]
01.Uninstall previous versions.
02.Install the software.
03.Run our keygen.
04.Select a product.
05.Push "Automatic Registration (Windows)".
06.Enjoy!

[MacOSX]
01.Uninstall previous versions.
02.Install the software.
03.Make your computer offline.
04.Run Authorization Manager.
05.Start offline authorization.
06.Save authorization file.
07.Run our keygen on Windows.
08.Select a product.
09.Generate a Serial Number.
10.Click "Get From Authorization Requset File" and open a request file (*.html).
11.Push "Generate For Other Computers" and save the authorization file.
12.Open the generated file with Authorization Manager.
13.Enjoy!


NOTE :
To activate Syntronik and Philharmonik2, you also need to activate SampleTank3.
All current sample based products are based on SampleTank3.
